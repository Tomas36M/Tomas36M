### — I. &nbsp;Who I am

Full stack developer with **2+ years of professional experience**, specialized in **TypeScript**
end to end: Next.js and React on the front, NestJS and Node on the back, PostgreSQL underneath.

What drives me is the line between **product and AI**: taking an LLM from prototype to
something that holds up with real users — cost, latency, permissions and edge cases actually
solved. I've worked with multi-model integrations (Claude, GPT, Gemini through LangChain),
voice transcription with Deepgram, document processing pipelines, and healthcare
interoperability with FHIR/HL7.

I also take design seriously: if an interface feels generic, it isn't finished yet.
